Common questions about submitting your video for the AI Theme Song & Video Challenge, part of the Periyar 148 celebrations.

### Who can participate?

Any youth participant who registered for the Periyar 148 Event Registration and selected the AI Theme Song & Video Challenge, and attended the AI workshop on August 30, 2026.

### What theme should I choose?

Pick one theme from the event topics: Social Justice, Equality, Scientific Future, Inventions, or Education. Your video should connect to your chosen theme in whatever way feels authentic to you.

### What style or AI tools can I use?

Any style, and any AI tool you like — video, voice, animation, or music generators are all fair game. Nothing is off-limits as long as the final result is your own original creative work.

### How long should the video be?

Shorter is fine — aim for up to 60 seconds. There's no minimum; a tight, well-made 20-30 second video is just as welcome as a full-minute one.

### What file format should I submit?

MP4 or MOV are preferred. Other common video formats are accepted too.

### How do I submit?

Use the [AI Project Video Submission form](https://form.jotform.com/262413731318048). You'll need the Registration ID and email address from your original Periyar 148 registration — both are included in the confirmation email you received, and also in the "Submit Your Video" email sent after the workshop.

### When is the deadline?

Submissions are due by **end of day, September 11, 2026**.

### How are videos judged, and when are results announced?

After submissions close, all entries are reviewed by our panel of judges. Results and special prizes are announced at the **Periyar 148 Birthday Celebration on September 19, 2026**. Selected participants will be notified in advance and asked to join a live Zoom session that day, where their video will be played and they'll get to share their experience with the judges.

### Can my video also be shared publicly?

That's entirely optional. The submission form includes a separate checkbox for permission to publish your video on the Periyar Vision YouTube channel — you can say yes or no, and it won't affect judging either way.

### I have another question — who do I contact?

Reply to any of our event emails or write to [events@periyarinternational.org](mailto:events@periyarinternational.org).
