### Download the Slides

Each presenter covered a different piece of the AI content creation journey — from understanding how AI actually works, to polishing a finished video for social media. All three sessions are available below as PDF downloads.

1. **[AI: Powerful Creative Teammate](/images/blog/downloads/ai-for-young-creators-workshop.pdf)** — presented by Arunachalam Lakshmanan
   How AI makes predictions, why it can get things wrong, and how to use it wisely as a creative partner for images, video, and music.

2. **[From Finished Video to Engaging Reel](/images/blog/downloads/ai-video-captions-and-effects-workshop.pdf)** — presented by Srikanth Daggumalli
   A live CapCut demo covering captions, effects, intros/outros, and the final polish that turns a finished video into a scroll-stopping short.

3. **[Go Viral in 60 Seconds](/images/blog/downloads/go-viral-in-60-seconds-workshop.pdf)** — presented by Akila Selvaraj
   How to make your own short film with AI — no camera, no editing software, and no prior experience needed.

### Quick Reference — Your Video

Ready to put what you learned into practice? Here's what to keep in mind while creating your submission:

- **Pick a theme** from the event topics: Social Justice, Equality, Scientific Future, Inventions, or Education.
- **Any style works** — use whichever AI tools you like to bring your idea to life.
- **Keep it short** — shorter is fine, aim for up to 60 seconds.
- **Be original** — creativity is your only limit!
